The Bliss Cakkery

Brand Identity, Packaging & Social Media Design

Industry
Bakery & Confectionery
Services Provided
Brand Identity Packaging Design Social Media Design

The Challenge

The Bliss Cakkery relied heavily on social media and local word-of-mouth for business. However, their digital presence lacked a strong, premium identity. Despite their products being of exceptionally high confectionery quality, their branding did not fully communicate that value, resulting in missed growth opportunities.

The Approach

We developed a comprehensive solution that elevates the brand at every customer touchpoint, split into three key pillars:

  • Brand Identity System: A refined visual framework centered on artisanal elegance, pairing sophisticated typography with a warm, minimalist color palette.
  • Packaging Design: High-end mockups and print guidelines designed to turn the box opening into a premium, sensory unboxing experience.
  • Social Media Visual Direction: Polished templates and grid strategies designed to showcase the pastries as fine art and build customer trust online.

Brand Identity

The identity centers on a custom serif logotype and clean typesetting, projecting elegance and culinary craft. The color palette blends creamy neutrals with gold and warm accents to make the confectionery feel premium and warm.

  • Logo: High-contrast modern serif wordmark.
  • Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans and Inter clean font system.
  • Color Palette: Cream, warm brown, and soft gold accents.

Packaging Design

We designed packaging that elevates the customer unboxing experience and increases perceived brand value. Using minimalist box structures, elegant sleeves, and custom labels, the physical product acts as an extension of the premium brand promise, turning first-time customers into brand advocates.

Social Media Visual Direction

The social media direction establishes brand consistency across all marketing outputs. By focusing on editorial product framing, sophisticated typography overlays, and a curated color palette, the Instagram posts build instant authority, driving higher digital trust and local customer recognition.
